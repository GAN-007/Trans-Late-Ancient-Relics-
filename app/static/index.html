What this system is — and is not

    
ESHB is a modern reversible bridge for English/Swahili spelling and IPA. It is deliberately separate from historical Egyptian.

    
Middle Egyptian mode teaches the ancient language. Historical inscriptions use consonantal phonograms, multi-consonant signs, logograms, determinatives, phonetic complements and artistic sign grouping. Vowels are not normally written, so exact Middle Egyptian pronunciation is not fully recoverable.

    
Translation policy: the software reports confidence and ambiguity. It does not invent Egyptian words when the lexicon or grammar does not support them.
